Privacy Policy

PETFLIX & CHILL Automation · Last updated 23 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the PETFLIX & CHILL Automation tool ("the App", "we", "us"), operated by Enfinity, accesses and handles data. The App is a private, internal tool used only by us to operate our own YouTube channels. It is not offered to the public and does not collect personal information from third-party end users.

Information the App accesses

Through Google and YouTube APIs, and only for accounts we own and have connected, the App accesses:

  • Our YouTube channel content (video list and metadata) in order to upload and manage our videos;
  • Our video titles, descriptions, tags, and thumbnails, which the App sets and updates;
  • Our own channel and video analytics (views, watch time, click-through rate, and similar performance metrics) to help us schedule and improve our content.

The App does not access, request, or store data belonging to any account other than our own connected channels.

How the App uses this information

  • To upload compilation videos to our channels and set their metadata and thumbnails;
  • To list, review, and manage videos on our own channels;
  • To read our own analytics so the App can help decide topics, formats, and posting times.

Data storage and retention

Operational data (such as our clip library, video metadata, and generated records) is stored in our own infrastructure (Supabase) and processing is performed by our automation server and by Anthropic's API for text/vision processing. Access tokens are stored securely and used only to operate our connected channels. We retain data only as long as needed to operate the channels and delete it when no longer required.

Data sharing

We do not sell or share this data with third parties. Data is processed only by the service providers that run the App on our behalf (e.g., Supabase for storage, Anthropic for AI processing) and by Google/YouTube as part of the API services the App uses.

YouTube API Services

The App uses YouTube API Services. By connecting a channel you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and the App's use of information received from YouTube APIs adheres to the YouTube API Services Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. You can review or revoke the App's access to your Google account at any time at Google security settings.
