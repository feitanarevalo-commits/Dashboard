PETFLIX & CHILL — Automation

Internal tool for producing and managing our animal-compilation YouTube channels

What this application does

PETFLIX & CHILL Automation is a private, internal tool operated by Enfinity to create, publish, and manage short animal-compilation videos on our own YouTube channels. It assembles video compilations from our licensed clip library, uploads them to our channels, sets titles/descriptions/thumbnails, and reads our channels' performance analytics to help schedule and improve our content.

It is not a public-facing product and does not serve or collect data from third-party end users.

How it uses YouTube

The tool uses YouTube API Services solely to operate our own channels: uploading videos, updating video metadata and thumbnails, listing and managing our videos, and reading our own channel analytics.
